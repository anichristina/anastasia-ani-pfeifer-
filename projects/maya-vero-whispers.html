Maya Vero “Whispers”

A song about being watched, whispered over and never quite left alone, turned into a film. Produced by Raw Eve Collective, “Whispers” is the title track from Maya Vero’s EP Frame, a collaboration between a group of female creatives. Maya Vero, a Netherlands-based artist known for deep ballads and emotional writing, gives voice to the weight of feeling constantly overwhelmed. The video lets that pressure take shape, quietly and unmistakably.

Directed, edited and created by Ani. A Raw Eve Collective production.
