Swim – Löna

Löna, the Utrecht-based dark-pop duo, step into a new era and trade purple for blue. “Swim” answers with an ethereal world to match: soft light, drifting fabric and two faces moving as one. Beyond the film, the project grew into the cover art and a series of visual teasers that carry the new colour through every release.

Concept, production, direction and editing by Ani.
